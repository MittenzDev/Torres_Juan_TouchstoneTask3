Our Story

Founded in 2001, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small network of foster homes committed to saving at-risk shelter animals across the city. Over the past 25 years, we have opened our doors to include dogs, cats, small pets like guinea pigs, rabbits and habitat animals.

Our Values

Every decision is guided by our committment to animal welfare and community trust:

  • Compassion First: Every animal in our care receives individual attention, affection, and respect.
  • Transparency: We maintain clear and honest communication in regards to medical needs, behavioral assessments, and shelter operations.
  • Lifelong Committment: We continue our support past adoption, offering post-adoption resources to ensure a health life for your new furry companion.
  • Community Partnership: We work together with local vets, foster families, and volunteers to maximize our impact.

Our Organization

Twin Cities Animal Rescue relies on a dedicate team of staff and network of volunteer leaders to help keep our programs running smoothly:

  • Rescue Operations Director: Oversees animal intake, shelter coordination, and medical care.
  • Foster and Adoption Coordinators: Match animals with potential adopters and manage foster placements.
  • Volunteer Coordinators: Recruit, train, and support community members across shelter activities.
