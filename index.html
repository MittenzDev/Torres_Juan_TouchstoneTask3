Our Mission Statement

Since 2001, Twin Cities Animal Rescue has been committed to saving vulnerable animals, providing expert medical care, and matching these rescued pets with their loving forever homes.


Learn More

Our Community Impact

Thanks to our caring supporters, fosters, and volunteers, we have been able to achieve:

  • 1200+ animals rescued and rehabilitated in the last year
  • 90% adoption rate across all of our facilities
  • 500 foster families providing temporary homes
  • 3000 volunteer hours supporting daily operations

Feature Rescue Initiative

We also provide specialized service and companion animal placement, matching rescue animals with people of all abilities.

How You Can Help

Whether you have time to give or room to spare in your home, your support saves lives each and every day.

  • Adopt: Give a rescue animal a loving forever home.
  • Foster: Temporarily open your home to animals awaiting adoption.
  • Volunteer: By assisting at our shelter, transporting pets, or helping out at community events.
